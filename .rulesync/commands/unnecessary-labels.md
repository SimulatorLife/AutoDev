---
targets: ["*"]
description: Identify and prioritize redundant or confusing user-facing labels.
---

Identify 5 obvious, high-priority instances of unnecessary UI text or labels after a reasonable review. Focus on text that is redundant, duplicates information already shown or implied elsewhere, is overly verbose, or adds more clutter/confusion than value. Remove or consolidate the text, and update any affected tests or documentation. If the change is not obvious, provide a clear rationale for why the text was removed or consolidated.
