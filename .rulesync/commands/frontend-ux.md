# Audit and improve frontend UI/UX

Audit, test, and improve the frontend/UI/dashboard for usability, consistency, maintainability, and UX best practices. Evaluate visual hierarchy, alignment, proximity, spacing, contrast, typography, balance, consistency, affordances, feedback, accessibility, responsiveness, and interaction patterns. Identify redundant information, unnecessary complexity, visual clutter, inconsistent styling, confusing workflows, and usability issues. Apply **DRY, KISS, separation of concerns, and established design-system principles**; consolidate duplicated UI logic, components, and styles into shared, reusable components, design tokens, and common styling patterns. Favor simplicity, clarity, predictable behavior, and progressive disclosure over unnecessary controls or text. Prioritize and fix high-impact issues, preserve existing functionality, and validate changes through appropriate UI, interaction, accessibility, and responsive-layout testing.
