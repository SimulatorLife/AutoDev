description: Review one document in the codebase to identify areas for improvement.
---

Review one documententation-document in the codebase to identify and fix/improve **at least 5** instances of issues from amongst these categories:

1. Overly verbose language/wording is unnecessary, makes things *less* clear, and can be made more concise.
2. Important/useful/necessary details is missing/omitted and should be considered for adding.
3. Content/wording/section duplicates another part of the document or another document in the codebase and should be removed/combined.
4. Bad organization. Disparate things should be grouped into one section, sections that should be combined, things that belong in another section and moved.
5. Paragraphs that should be converted into a list or table to improve readability, conciseness, and scannability.
6. Things that are ambiguous, contradictory, or lack clarity, or are so vague that they are essentially meaningless and add no/less value as-is.
7. Inaccurate, outdated, or misleading information that should be corrected or removed.

When done, perform one final review to ensure that all identified issues have been addressed and that no important/useful/necessary details from the original document(s) was removed/omitted and should be restored.
